A Stent Fixes 48 Millimeters; Cholesterol Affects the Entire Body

Why heart attack patients suffer recurrent events in other vessels

Local Treatment

The Stent is a Mechanical Scaffold

  • Treats exactly 48 mm of the mid-LAD.
  • Restores acute lumen diameter and flow.
  • Zero effect on the rest of the cardiovascular system.
Local salvage ≠ Systemic cure
Systemic Reality

Cholesterol Drives the Next Rupture

  • LDL of 173 mg/dL continues depositing plaque across all arteries.
  • Mr. Hong already has a 40-50% stenosis in his proximal LAD!
  • PROSPECT Trial: 50% of recurrent heart attacks occur at non-culprit lesions, not the stented site!
Plaque stabilization prevents the next attack!

The Target: What is Mr. Hong's Goal?

International guidelines mandate a strict target for secondary prevention

Guideline Standard

The Post-MI Rule: Two Targets

1. ≥ 50% Reduction

Cut his baseline LDL in half.

2. Absolute LDL < 55 mg/dL

Strict secondary prevention threshold.

Source: 2026 ACC/AHA Dyslipidemia Guideline & ESC Guidelines

The Evidence

Why Must We Go Below 55?

  • Fewer Heart Attacks: Every 39 mg/dL drop in LDL cuts major vascular events by 22%.
  • Plaque Reversal: Coronary ultrasound shows plaques only shrink when LDL drops below 60-70 mg/dL.
  • Proven Safety: Trials treating down to 30 mg/dL showed no safety issues.
✓ Lower is better, and earlier is better!

1. Blumenthal RS, et al. 2026 ACC/AHA Dyslipidemia Guideline. Circulation. 2026. PMID: 41824552.
2. Mach F, et al. 2019 ESC/EAS Guidelines for the management of dyslipidaemias. Eur Heart J. 2020;41(1):111-188.

The Solution: Combine Statin with Ezetimibe

Targeting both liver production and gut absorption closes the gap

Pillar 1: Liver

High-Intensity Statin

  • Action: Blocks cholesterol production in the liver.
  • Effect: Pulls LDL out of the bloodstream.
  • Dose: Atorvastatin 80 mg or Rosuvastatin 20-40 mg.
  • Drop: Lowers LDL by ∼50%.
Pillar 2: Gut

Ezetimibe 10 mg

  • Action: Blocks cholesterol absorption in the intestine.
  • Synergy: Adds another 15-20% drop!
  • IMPROVE-IT Trial: In 18,000 post-MI patients, reached LDL 54 mg/dL and prevented heart attacks.
✓ Statin + Ezetimibe delivers ~65% total reduction — reaching < 55 mg/dL! Target Achieved

1. Cannon CP, et al. IMPROVE-IT Trial. N Engl J Med. 2015;372(25):2387-2397. PMID: 26039521.
2. Kim BK, et al. RACING Trial. Lancet. 2022;400(10349):380-390. PMID: 35863373.

What If LDL Remains High? Add a PCSK9 Inhibitor

The powerful third line of defense for very-high-risk patients

Pillar 3

How PCSK9 Inhibitors Work

  • The Problem: PCSK9 destroys LDL receptors on liver cells.
  • The Drug: Injections (Evolocumab / Alirocumab / Inclisiran) block PCSK9.
  • Result: Liver cells keep their receptors and rapidly clear cholesterol from the blood!
  • Power: Adds another 50% to 60% reduction.
Trial Evidence

FOURIER & ODYSSEY Trials

  • Massive Drop: Lowered median LDL to 30-40 mg/dL.
  • Event Reduction: 15% fewer heart attacks and strokes.
  • Survival: Significantly reduced overall mortality in high-baseline patients!
Triple therapy achieves >80% overall LDL drop!

1. Sabatine MS, et al. FOURIER Trial. N Engl J Med. 2017;376:1713-1722. PMID: 28304224.
2. Schwartz GG, et al. ODYSSEY OUTCOMES Trial. N Engl J Med. 2018;379:2097-2107. PMID: 30403574.
