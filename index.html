2026 ACC/AHA Guideline: The Dual Target

2026 最新指引極高風險二級預防雙重門檻 ｜ Dual Goals: ≥ 50% Reduction AND Absolute LDL < 55 mg/dL

Guideline Mandate ｜ 指引強制標準

The Dual Clinical Targets (雙重達標準則)

≥ 50% Relative Reduction 基準值相對降幅
< 55 Target LDL (mg/dL) 絕對值目標 (< 1.4 mmol/L)
  • Rule 1 (Relative): Lower baseline LDL by at least 50%. 第一準則：必須從基準數值降低至少 50%。
  • Rule 2 (Absolute): Reach absolute circulating LDL-C < 55 mg/dL. 第二準則：絕對數值必須壓至 55 mg/dL 以下。
  • Very-High-Risk ASCVD: Post-STEMI patients require immediate dual compliance. STEMI 患者屬於極高風險族群，必須兩者同時滿足。
Both criteria must be fulfilled simultaneously 相對降幅與絕對數值缺一不可，是極高風險患者的標準防線
Clinical Evidence ｜ 斑塊逆轉實證

Why Target < 55 mg/dL? (為何必須低於 55？)

Plaque Regression Threshold: < 55 mg/dL

血管內超音波 (IVUS) 證實：只有降至 55 以下，斑塊體積才會實質縮小。

  • Lower is Better: Every 38.7 mg/dL (1 mmol/L) drop cuts major vascular events by 22% (CTT). 統合分析：每降 38.7 mg/dL，重大心血管事件減少 22%。
  • Cap Thickening: Low LDL thickens fibrous caps, turning vulnerable lipid into stable scar tissue. 深度降脂能顯著增厚纖維帽，將易碎斑塊轉化為穩定疤痕組織。
✓ Halts plaque growth and induces true regression 有效終止動脈斑塊進展，實現臨床影像學上的真正逆轉

1. 2026 ACC/AHA Guideline on the Management of Blood Cholesterol. Circulation. 2026;153(4):e112-e198.
2. Baigent C, et al. Efficacy and Safety of More Intensive Lowering of LDL Cholesterol (CTT Collaboration). Lancet. 2010;376(9753):1670-1681. PMID: 21067804.

Non-Pharmacological Therapy: The Lifestyle Foundation

非藥物治療：高血脂與心血管照護的堅實基石 ｜ Evidence-Based Diet, Exercise, and Rehabilitation

Nutritional Strategy ｜ 降脂營養策略

1. Dietary Modulation (高血脂飲食處方)

  • Mediterranean Dietary Pattern: Emphasize EVOO, nuts, vegetables, and legumes (PREDIMED: 30% MACE drop). 地中海飲食：特級初榨橄欖油、堅果與豆類，減少 30% 心血管事件。
  • Saturated & Trans Fats: Saturated fat < 7% daily calories; eliminate trans fats. 飽和脂肪嚴格限制於每日總熱量 7% 以下，徹底杜絕反式脂肪。
  • Viscous Soluble Fiber: 10–25 g/day from oats and psyllium binds intestinal bile acids. 每日 10–25 克水溶性膳食纖維（燕麥、洋車前子），促進膽鹽排出（降 5–10% LDL）。
  • Plant Stanols / Sterols: 2 g/day competitively displaces cholesterol absorption. 每日 2 克植物固醇，競爭性抑制小腸膽固醇吸收（降 8–12% LDL）。
Dietary synergy lowers LDL by 10%–15% and improves vascular endothelium 綜合飲食介入可降低約 10%–15% 膽固醇，並大幅修復血管內皮機能
Rehabilitation ｜ 復健與行為介入

2. Cardiac Rehab & Lifestyle (心臟復健與習慣)

  • Phase I to II Cardiac Rehab: Exercise training reduces post-MI mortality by 26%. 一期住院至二期門診心臟復健：運動訓練降低 26% 總死亡率。
  • Aerobic Activity: ≥ 150 min/week moderate exercise raises HDL and lowers TG. 每週 ≥ 150 分鐘中強度有氧運動，提高保護性 HDL 並降低三酸甘油酯。
  • Complete Smoking Cessation: Halves recurrent infarction risk within 1 year. 徹底戒菸：終止內皮氧化壓力，一年內再發心梗風險直接減半。
  • Metabolic Control: BMI < 24 kg/m² to reduce insulin resistance and vascular inflammation. 體重控制：維持 BMI < 24，減輕胰島素阻抗與全身血管慢性發炎。
✓ Foundational for life, but diet alone cannot bridge a 118 mg/dL gap 生活型態是終生健康基石，但單靠飲食運動無法跨越洪先生 118 mg/dL 的治療缺口

From Acute to Chronic: Mr. Hong's Complete Journey

從急性期到慢性期：洪先生的全方位醫療歷程 ｜ Synchronized Lifestyle, Cardiac Rehab, and Stepwise Therapy

Phase 1: Inpatient ｜ 急性住院期

Acute Phase (Day 0–3)

  • Rehab: Inpatient Phase I physical mobilization. 展開第一期住院復健活動。

Phase 2: Post-Discharge ｜ 亞急性門診

Subacute Phase (Wk 4–6)

  • Lipid Panel: Measure fasting response at 4–6 weeks. 4 到 6 週回診抽血追蹤血脂。
  • Target Evaluation: Confirm if LDL < 55 mg/dL. 評估是否順利達標 < 55 mg/dL。
  • Stepwise Escalation: If LDL ≥ 55, add PCSK9i. 若未達標，立即升級 PCSK9 抑制劑。

  • Plaque Stability: Lifelong plaque quiescence. 長期維持斑塊穩定，實現零復發存活。
Lifelong plaque quiescence & zero recurrence 目標：終身斑塊穩定無破裂，重拾健康人生
